WEST AFRICA · NIGERIA

Jollof Rice

Jollof rice is a beloved West African dish cooked in a rich sauce made with tomatoes, peppers, onions, and aromatic spices. It is commonly enjoyed at family gatherings and celebrations.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of long-grain rice
  • 4 tomatoes, blended
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons of tomato paste
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt and spices to taste
  • Stock or water
View cooking instructions
  1. Blend the tomatoes and red bell pepper until smooth.
  2. Heat oil in a pot and fry the onion and tomato paste.
  3. Add the blended mixture and cook until the sauce thickens.
  4. Season the sauce and add stock or water.
  5. Add the washed rice and stir gently.
  6. Cover and cook on low heat until the rice is tender, adding a little liquid if necessary.

NORTH AFRICA · MOROCCO

Moroccan Tagine

Moroccan tagine is known for its slow-cooked flavors and fragrant spices. It often combines vegetables with chicken, lamb, or legumes to create a comforting meal.

Ingredients

  • 500 g of chicken or cooked chickpeas
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 carrots, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • Cumin
  • Ground ginger and turmeric
  • A pinch of cinnamon
  • Olive oil, salt, and stock or water
View cooking instructions
  1. Heat olive oil in a heavy pot.
  2. Add the onions and garlic and cook until softened.
  3. Add the chicken or chickpeas, vegetables, and spices.
  4. Pour in a little stock or water.
  5. Cover and simmer gently until the ingredients are tender and the flavors have combined.
  6. Serve warm with bread or couscous.

EAST AFRICA · RWANDA

Isombe

Isombe is a traditional Rwandan dish prepared with cassava leaves. The leaves are cooked thoroughly and may be combined with groundnuts, onions, and other ingredients to create a flavorful meal.

Ingredients

  • 500 g of prepared cassava leaves
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • Groundnut paste, if desired
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed
View cooking instructions
  1. Use cassava leaves that have been properly prepared for cooking.
  2. Cook the leaves thoroughly in water until tender.
  3. Add the chopped onion, garlic, and a little cooking oil.
  4. Stir in groundnut paste if desired and add water as needed.
  5. Continue cooking until the ingredients are well combined and fully cooked.
  6. Serve with ugali, rice, or another preferred accompaniment.

SOUTHERN AFRICA · SOUTH AFRICA

Bunny Chow

Bunny chow is a famous South African street-food dish associated with Durban. It features a flavorful curry seasoned with aromatic spices and served inside a hollowed-out loaf of bread.

Ingredients

  • 1 small loaf of white bread
  • 500 g of chicken, beans, or potatoes
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • Garlic and ginger
  • Curry powder
  • Cooking oil and salt
  • Water or stock
View cooking instructions
  1. Heat oil in a pot and fry the onion, garlic, and ginger.
  2. Add the curry powder and stir briefly.
  3. Add the chicken, beans, or potatoes and the chopped tomatoes.
  4. Pour in a little water or stock and simmer until everything is cooked.
  5. Cut the loaf into portions and hollow out the center.
  6. Fill the bread with the curry and serve warm.
